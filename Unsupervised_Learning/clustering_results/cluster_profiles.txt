COMPARISON: K-MEANS vs GMM
================================================================

  Aspect                    | K-Means (k=2)              | GMM (n=8)
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Optimal config            | k=2 (Elbow+Silhouette+DB)  | n=8 (BIC)
  Assignment type           | Hard (binary label)         | Soft (probability)
  Silhouette Score          | 0.6847 (excellent)          | -0.0057 (overlapping)
  Davies-Bouldin            | 1.0216 (compact)            | 5.2976
  Top group fraud rate      | 0.3439% (2.06×)             | 8.6439% (51.8×)
  Fraud cases in top group  | 18 / 473  (3.8%)            | 385 / 473 (81.4%)
  Uncertainty representation| None                        | Explicit (0–1 score)
  Legitimate diversity      | 1 cluster                   | 7 distinct sub-patterns
  Key integration output    | Cluster label → threshold   | GMM_FraudProba → XGBoost

  Conclusion:
  K-Means and GMM are complementary algorithms. K-Means provides the
  clean structural baseline and dynamic threshold logic. GMM provides
  the probabilistic depth, the granular fraud signature (Component 0),
  and the continuous GMM_FraudProba score that together power the
  integration strategies in Section 7.
